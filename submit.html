Submit your work

Espero welcomes submissions from students. We aim to provide a platform for students to explore medicine, science and healthcare from different perspectives, bringing together original research, emerging ideas and critical discussion.

Whether you are interested in presenting your research, discussing an issue in healthcare or exploring an emerging development in medicine, we encourage you to submit your work.

What can you submit?

We are very flexible in terms of submissions. We accept a range of written contributions, including, but not limited to:

  • Original Research: Original studies, research projects and scientific findings.
  • Literature Reviews: Critical reviews and discussions of existing scientific literature.
  • Perspectives & Opinion: Essays and opinion pieces addressing medicine, healthcare, ethics and society.
  • Scientific Features: Articles exploring emerging developments, discoveries and innovations in medicine and science.
  • Interviews: Conversations with clinicians, researchers, academics and other experts.
  • Case Reports: Clinical case reports and discussions, subject to appropriate ethical approval and patient confidentiality requirements.

Submission Guidelines

Before submitting, please ensure that:

  • Your submission is original and has not been published elsewhere.
  • Scientific claims are supported by appropriate evidence and references.
  • Any research involving human participants has received the necessary ethical approval and consent.
  • Patient information is appropriately anonymised and confidentiality is maintained.

We welcome submissions from students at all stages of their academic careers.

Submission Instructions

To submit your manuscript, email your article as a Word document, including the following information:

  • Your name
  • Graduation year
  • Type of submission

Email your submission

Not using Gmail? Open your mail app, or write to esperoumb@gmail.com and attach your article.
